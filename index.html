Today's lesson: Ohm's Law

Voltage (V) pushes charge, current (I) is flow of charge, and resistance (R) opposes current.

Formula: V = I × R; I = V/R; R = V/I.

Example: 12 V across 4 Ω gives I = 12/4 = 3 A.

Normal target: 5 questions. Holiday mode: 10 questions plus extra revision.

📘 Learn step by step

Lesson 1 — Ohm's Law

  • Voltage unit: volt (V)
  • Current unit: ampere (A)
  • Resistance unit: ohm (Ω)
  • For an ohmic element under constant conditions: V = IR.

Worked examples

  1. V=10 V, R=5 Ω → I=2 A.
  2. I=2 A, R=6 Ω → V=12 V.
  3. V=24 V, I=3 A → R=8 Ω.
